Hi, we're beast!

We're the graduate student association for the joint UC Berkeley-UCSF Bioengineering Graduate Program. Our organization is dedicated to helping students navigate the graduate school process across half a decade and two sides of the Bay. Our members help plan the interviewee Visit Weekend, create social events for the entire program, and sit on the UCSF-UC Berkeley Bioengineering admissions committee.

Contact Us

For general student-led help with classes, rotations or dealing with lab, please reach out to our Peer Advisors. For administrative and logistics help, visit our FAQs page, or reach out to Rocio and Victoria.

For concerns related to Beast events and affairs, take a look at our member directory below to get your email to the right person quickly, or leave us a general message in the form.
